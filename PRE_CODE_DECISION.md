## Brand read and dials

`A one-to-one Romiley dog groomer whose own aqua-and-blush artwork sets a distinct visual language, while every foreground customer image remains complete and unaltered.`

- Lazy default rejected: pastel paw-print cards, cartoon icons and a generic dog-stock-photo hero.
- Soul: affectionate local editorial, grounded by complete real groomed-dog photography and the recorded logo artwork's aqua, blush and hand-drawn details. 
- SOUL 9: playful but specific local character; SPECTACLE 4: static colour fields, asymmetrical logo stage and soft background treatment; DENSITY 6: useful service and proof information without a crowded platform listing.
- Finesse-ui influence: premium substrate, a locked palette, grain, concise display type and one CSS-only hero treatment. Design-taste-frontend influence: a single purposeful booking CTA, four layout families, no repeated eyebrow architecture or card grid. Media-use influence: the asset ledger and source-bound, unaltered local asset workflow; no provider/login/install action was used.

## Two materially different directions considered

1. **Paws-at-play pastel catalogue**: bright colour blocks, service tiles and a tiled gallery. Rejected as a repeatable dog-grooming template that weakens the calm 1-to-1 proposition.
2. **Dog-first local editorial**: a complete, original-ratio dog photo and category label in the opening view, followed by portfolio, services and proof. Selected because it makes the category clear in seconds without altering customer imagery.
